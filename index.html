FAQ

Questions before you buy

How do I buy a car from Dharti Motors?

Browse the cars, open one you like and book a test drive. We’ll confirm a time with you on Instagram.

Can I see the car before I decide?

Yes. Test drives are by appointment. Message us and we’ll arrange a time in Ahmedabad.

Do you help with a car loan?

Yes. Use the EMI estimate on each car for a rough idea, and message us for finance options.

Which documents are needed to buy?

Usually ID proof, address proof and PAN. We’ll tell you the exact list when you enquire.

Do you handle RC transfer and insurance?

We help with ownership transfer and insurance. Message us to confirm the steps for the car you choose.

How do I sell my car to you?

Fill in the Sell your car form. We’ll check the car and share an offer.
